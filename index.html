Core Features

Obstacle & Hazard Navigation

Real-time detection of obstacles in your path, from low-lying trip hazards to overhead obstacles. Alerts are spatially accurate, telling you exactly where to step.

Street Crossing Assistance

Monitors traffic signals and moving vehicles. Counts down crossing time and alerts you to approaching cars from any direction.

Scene/Text/Object Recognition

Reads signs, menus, labels, and documents in real time. Identifies objects by name and describes scenes with relevant detail.

Facial Recognition

Securely recognize known contacts by face. IRIS announces when someone you know approaches, with privacy-first design that keeps data on-device.
